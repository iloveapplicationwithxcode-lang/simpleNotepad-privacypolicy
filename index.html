Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how xNotePad ("the App") handles your information.

Information Collection and Use

The App does not require an account. We do not collect, receive, or store personal information or the contents of your notes on our servers. The App does not include advertising or analytics tools that collect information about your use of the App.

Notes and Local Storage

The notes you create are stored locally on your device so that you can view and edit them. The App does not transmit your notes to us or to an external service.

Your device's operating system may back up or restore app data depending on your device settings and the App's backup configuration. Any such backup is handled by your operating system or backup provider under its own privacy policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your notes or personal information. If you choose to copy, export, or share a note using features available on your device, the destination app or service handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Notes remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App's locally stored data. You can remove locally stored app data through your device's settings or by uninstalling the App. Copies you have exported, shared, or backed up may remain elsewhere and must be managed separately.

Security

Your notes are protected by the security features available on your device. We recommend using a device screen lock and keeping your operating system up to date. No method of storage is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the support contact information provided on the App's store listing.
